The Industry Gap

Retail is bleeding money on guesswork

Fit uncertainty costs the fashion industry billions every year — online and in-store alike. Visiwear removes the guesswork entirely.

The Friction

Where fashion retail loses today

Every uncertain purchase creates a chain of cost — from the courier to the warehouse to the landfill.

  • ✕
    High cost of e-commerce returns Free returns feel customer-friendly, but each one erodes margin through reverse logistics, re-packaging and refund processing.
  • ✕
    Unsellable damaged inventory Returned garments frequently come back worn, stained or without tags — no longer sellable at full price, often written off entirely.
  • ✕
    Consumer online purchase hesitation Shoppers abandon carts because they can't see how a garment drapes on their own body shape or whether the fit will be right.
  • ✕
    The In-Store Sizing Deficit Physical stores lose direct sales when a customer wants an item that is out of stock in their size — the sale walks out the door.
The Solution

One automated omni-channel ecosystem

Visiwear uses advanced computer-vision AI to let users try on garments and check fits before buying — online or on the shop floor.

  • ✓
    Automated omni-channel ecosystem A single AI engine powers both your web storefront and your physical retail floor — consistent fit intelligence everywhere your customer shops.
  • ✓
    Phase 2 Virtual Try-On Station An in-store interactive station lets shoppers virtually try garments and buy clothes with absolute confidence — even when their size isn't on the rail.
  • ✓
    Phase 2 Intelligent Size Recommendation Visiwear automatically calculates and recommends the optimal garment size that perfectly fits the user's specific dimensions — no measuring tape, no guesswork.
